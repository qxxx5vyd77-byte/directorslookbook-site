Suno Reggaeton Prompt: Style and Exclude Lines

Hear the style, 10 s: Reggaeton

What it is

Puerto Rican club music built on the dembow rhythm: a kick on every beat with a snare pattern that skips in threes, deep sub bass and a smooth Spanish vocal.

Suno prompt

Two boxes, ready to paste. The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

reggaeton, 94 bpm dembow rhythm, booming kick on every beat, snappy syncopated snare, deep sub bass, minor synth plucks and a nylon guitar loop, smooth male lead vocal in Spanish, sultry late-night club feel

Exclude

rock guitar, trap hi-hat rolls, house build, EDM drop, orchestral, country, jazz chords, acoustic folk

The parts

The lead is the one instrument that carries the tune. Colour instruments add texture and never take the melody. The bed is the rhythm section underneath.

Tempo
94 bpm
Meter
4/4
Swing
straight
Key
A minor
Lead (carries the tune)
smooth male lead vocal in Spanish
Colour (never the melody)
nylon guitar loop, synth plucks
Bed
dembow kick and snare, deep sub bass

Not this

Not cumbia: the dembow snare pattern drives it, not a guiro and accordion. Not trap: the kick never drops out into half-time.

Other names

dembow, perreo, urbano
