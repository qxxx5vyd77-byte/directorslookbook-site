Suno Gospel Choir Prompt: Style and Exclude Lines

Hear the style, 10 s: Gospel Choir

What it is

African American church music: a full choir answering a lead singer, organ and piano trading chords, tambourine and hand claps, swaying in a 12/8 feel.

Suno prompt

Two boxes, ready to paste. The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

gospel choir, traditional church style, full choir call and response with a soaring lead vocal, gospel organ, rolling piano, tambourine and hand claps, joyful and rising, swaying 12/8 feel, 70 bpm

Exclude

rap, autotune, trap, EDM, plainchant, cathedral choir, opera, rock guitar, synth pad, lo-fi, children's choir

The parts

The lead is the one instrument that carries the tune. Colour instruments add texture and never take the melody. The bed is the rhythm section underneath.

Tempo
70 bpm (dotted-quarter pulse)
Meter
6/8
Swing
straight
Key
Ab major
Lead (carries the tune)
lead vocalist, the choir answers (needs lyrics: an empty box fires instrumental)
Colour (never the melody)
organ swells, tambourine
Bed
rolling piano, bass, drums and hand claps

Not this

Not a cathedral choir or plainchant: this is call and response with organ, piano and hand claps, raw and joyful.

Other names

church gospel, gospel praise

Pairs with

  • slow push-in on raised hands, warm backlight
  • warm golden film grain
  • a bold condensed serif title
