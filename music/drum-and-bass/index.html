Suno Drum and Bass Prompt: Style and Exclude Lines

Hear the style, 10 s: Drum and Bass

What it is

Fast UK breakbeat music near 174 bpm: chopped breaks or a two-step kick and snare over rolling sub bass. The liquid style adds soft pads and keys.

Suno prompt

Two boxes, ready to paste. The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

liquid drum and bass instrumental, fast chopped breakbeat drums, rolling detuned sub bass, warm atmospheric pads, soft electric piano chords, rushing but emotive, 174 bpm

Exclude

vocals, singing, rap, MC, dubstep wobble, half-time, trap, EDM festival drop, hardstyle, metal guitar, lo-fi, orchestral

The parts

The lead is the one instrument that carries the tune. Colour instruments add texture and never take the melody. The bed is the rhythm section underneath.

Tempo
174 bpm
Meter
4/4
Swing
straight
Key
F minor
Lead (carries the tune)
soft electric piano and pad line
Colour (never the melody)
atmospheric pads, rain texture
Bed
chopped breakbeat, rolling sub bass

Not this

Not dubstep: the drums never fall into a half-time wobble. Not hardcore: it rolls, it does not pound.

Other names

DnB, liquid DnB, jungle
