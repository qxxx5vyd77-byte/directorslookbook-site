Suno Vocal Trance Prompt: Style and Exclude Lines

Hear the style, 10 s: Vocal Trance

What it is

Early-2000s club anthem: long builds into big releases, a breathy female vocal floating over rolling offbeat bass, supersaw chords and a four-on-the-floor kick.

Suno prompt

Two boxes, ready to paste. The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

uplifting progressive vocal trance, early 2000s, breathy ethereal female vocal, warm supersaw pad chords, rolling offbeat bassline, gated synth plucks, four-on-the-floor kick, driving, euphoric and hopeful, 136 bpm

Exclude

rap, male vocal, belting, autotune, dubstep, wobble bass, big room, festival drop, cheap drop, hardstyle, trap hi-hats, electric guitar, lo-fi, vinyl crackle

The parts

The lead is the one instrument that carries the tune. Colour instruments add texture and never take the melody. The bed is the rhythm section underneath.

Bed

Not this

Not big-room EDM: no festival drop, no trap hats. Leave out the word deep: in this lane it returned 8-minute takes.

Other names
