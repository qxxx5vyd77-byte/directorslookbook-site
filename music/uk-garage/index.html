Suno UK Garage Prompt: Style and Exclude Lines

Hear the style, 10 s: UK Garage

What it is

Late-90s London club music: a shuffled two-step beat that skips the kick on beat three, rubbery sub bass, chopped organ stabs and warm electric piano.

Suno prompt

Two boxes, ready to paste. The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

UK garage instrumental, late 1990s 2-step beat, skippy shuffled hi-hats, crisp rimshots, rubbery sub bass, chopped organ chord stabs, warm electric piano, swaggering and bouncy, 132 bpm

Exclude

vocals, singing, rap, four-on-the-floor, house kick, drum and bass, dubstep, trap, EDM drop, rock guitar, orchestral, lo-fi

The parts

The lead is the one instrument that carries the tune. Colour instruments add texture and never take the melody. The bed is the rhythm section underneath.

Tempo
132 bpm
Meter
4/4
Swing
22% on the off-16ths
Key
G minor
Lead (carries the tune)
chopped organ and electric piano stabs
Colour (never the melody)
shuffled hats, rimshot fills
Bed
2-step kick and rimshot, rubbery sub bass

Not this

Not house: the kick is not four-on-the-floor. Not drum and bass: about 132 bpm and swung, not 174 and straight.

Other names

2-step garage, UKG
