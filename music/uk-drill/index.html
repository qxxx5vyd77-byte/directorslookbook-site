Suno UK Drill Prompt: Style and Exclude Lines

What it is

2010s London rap beat at a half-time 140s tempo: gliding 808 bass that bends between notes, skippy hi-hats and snares that land late, under dark minor piano or choir stabs.

Suno prompt

Two boxes, ready to paste. The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

UK drill beat, 142 bpm half-time feel, sliding 808 bass glides, skippy triplet hi-hats, late syncopated snares, dark minor piano and eerie choir stabs, low male rap vocal with a cold clipped flow

Exclude

sung pop chorus, bright major chords, boom bap drums, lo-fi crackle, guitar solo, EDM drop, reggaeton, comedy

The parts

The lead is the one instrument that carries the tune. Colour instruments add texture and never take the melody. The bed is the rhythm section underneath.

Not this

Not trap: the 808 slides and the snares arrive late instead of on beat three. Not grime: slower, darker, no square-wave bleeps.
