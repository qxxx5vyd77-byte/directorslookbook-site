Suno Hyperpop Prompt: Style and Exclude Lines

Hear the style, 10 s: Hyperpop

What it is

2010s internet pop pushed to the limit: pitched-up autotuned vocals, blown-out bass, sugary chords and sudden glitch edits that jump between styles.

Suno prompt

Two boxes, ready to paste. The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

hyperpop, 160 bpm, pitched-up autotuned vocals, glitchy maximal synths, blown-out distorted 808 bass, bubblegum chords, sudden genre switches and stutter edits, chaotic and euphoric

Exclude

acoustic, unplugged, slow ballad, jazz, orchestral, lo-fi, warm analog, country, restrained mix

The parts

The lead is the one instrument that carries the tune. Colour instruments add texture and never take the melody. The bed is the rhythm section underneath.

Tempo
160 bpm
Meter
4/4
Swing
straight
Key
C major
Lead (carries the tune)

Colour (never the melody)

Bed
distorted 808, maximal synths

Not this

Not synth-pop: nothing is tasteful or restrained. Not EDM: the song lurches and glitches instead of building to a drop.

Other names

glitch pop, digicore

Pairs with

  • jump cuts and rapid zooms
  • candy-pop oversaturated colour
  • a chrome bubble display title
