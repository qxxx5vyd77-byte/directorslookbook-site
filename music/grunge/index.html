Suno Grunge Prompt: Style and Exclude Lines

Hear the style, 10 s: Grunge

What it is

Early-1990s Pacific Northwest rock: sludgy detuned guitars, quiet verses that explode into loud choruses, heavy drums and a raspy, anguished voice.

Suno prompt

Two boxes, ready to paste. The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

early 90s grunge, 112 bpm, sludgy detuned distorted guitars, quiet verse into loud chorus, heavy pounding drums, thick bass, raspy anguished male vocal, raw garage recording

Exclude

synth, pop polish, autotune, EDM, rap, metal screams, shredding solos, orchestral, lo-fi beat, acoustic folk

The parts

The lead is the one instrument that carries the tune. Colour instruments add texture and never take the melody. The bed is the rhythm section underneath.

Tempo
112 bpm
Meter
4/4
Swing
straight
Key
D minor
Lead (carries the tune)
raspy male vocal
Colour (never the melody)
feedback, quiet clean-tone verses
Bed
detuned distorted guitars, heavy drums, thick bass

Not this

Not metal: no shredding solos or gallops. Not shoegaze: the vocal is up front and the guitars are dry and heavy.

Other names

90s grunge, Seattle sound
