Suno Shoegaze Prompt: Style and Exclude Lines

Hear the style, 10 s: Shoegaze

What it is

Early-90s guitar rock buried in its own noise: layers of reverb-soaked, pitch-bending fuzz guitars, buried breathy vocals and steady drums underneath.

Suno prompt

Two boxes, ready to paste. The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

shoegaze, early 1990s, walls of reverb-soaked fuzz guitars with tremolo-bar bends, buried wordless breathy vocals, steady mid-tempo drums, warm bass, dreamy and overwhelming, 112 bpm

Exclude

clear lead vocal, lyrics, pop vocal, rap, screaming, metal, grunge, crunchy distortion, EDM, synth pop, acoustic, lo-fi hip hop, autotune

The parts

The lead is the one instrument that carries the tune. Colour instruments add texture and never take the melody. The bed is the rhythm section underneath.

Tempo
112 bpm
Meter
4/4
Swing
straight
Key
E major
Lead (carries the tune)
fuzz guitar wall
Colour (never the melody)
buried breathy vocals, tremolo-bar bends
Bed
steady drums, warm bass

Not this

Not grunge or metal: the distortion is soft and smeared, never crunchy. Not dream pop: denser, louder guitar walls.

Other names

nu-gaze, dreamgaze

Pairs with

  • out-of-focus handheld into the sun
  • overexposed film with heavy halation
  • a soft lowercase sans title
