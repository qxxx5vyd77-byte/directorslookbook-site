Suno Music Box Prompt: Style and Exclude Lines

Hear the style, 10 s: Music Box

What it is

A comb of tuned steel teeth plucked by a turning cylinder: high, bell-like, a little out of tune, slowing as the spring runs down. Usually in waltz time.

Suno prompt

Two boxes, ready to paste. The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

wind-up music box melody, delicate plucked steel comb, waltz time, slightly detuned, soft mechanical clicks, faint room reverb, tender and fragile, gently slowing, 84 bpm

Exclude

vocals, singing, humming, choir, orchestra, drums, glockenspiel, celesta, christmas, sleigh bells, synth pad, lullaby, nursery

The parts

The lead is the one instrument that carries the tune. Colour instruments add texture and never take the melody. The bed is the rhythm section underneath.

Tempo
84 bpm
Meter
3/4
Swing
straight
Key
F major
Lead (carries the tune)
music box comb
Colour (never the melody)
mechanical clicks, room tone
Bed
none, or a soft felt piano underneath

Not this

Not a nursery track: keep sleep, baby and lullaby out of tags and titles, or the whole record gets filed as music for infants.

Other names

wind-up music box, toy music box
