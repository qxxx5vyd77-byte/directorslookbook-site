Suno Jersey Club Prompt: Style and Exclude Lines

Hear the style, 10 s: Jersey Club

What it is

2000s New Jersey dance music at 140: a kick pattern that stutters into quick triple hits, chopped vocal loops, sample squeaks and short chant hooks made for dance moves.

Suno prompt

Two boxes, ready to paste. The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

Jersey club, 140 bpm, bouncy kick pattern with rapid triple kicks, bed-squeak and chopped vocal sample loops, call-out chant vocals, heavy sub bass hits, playful dance-challenge energy

Exclude

slow tempo, half-time trap, guitar, orchestral, ballad, jazz chords, long intro, ambient pads

The parts

The lead is the one instrument that carries the tune. Colour instruments add texture and never take the melody. The bed is the rhythm section underneath.

Tempo
140 bpm
Meter

Swing
straight
Key

Bed

Not this

Not Baltimore club or footwork: a steady 140 with the signature triple kick. Not drill: bright and bouncy, no menace.

Other names

club bounce, bed squeak club
