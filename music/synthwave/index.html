Suno Synthwave Prompt: Style and Exclude Lines

Hear the style, 10 s: Synthwave

What it is

A 2010s revival of 80s film and arcade synth music: gated-reverb snare, pulsing arpeggiated analog bass, wide polysynth pads and a bright lead.

Suno prompt

Two boxes, ready to paste. The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

synthwave instrumental, 1980s retro analog sound, pulsing arpeggiated synth bass, gated reverb snare, drum machine, wide polysynth pads, bright sawtooth lead melody, night driving, steady 100 bpm

Exclude

vocals, singing, humming, chiptune, 8-bit, vaporwave, slowed samples, lo-fi, vinyl crackle, trap hi-hats, dubstep, EDM drop, orchestral, acoustic guitar, metal

The parts

The lead is the one instrument that carries the tune. Colour instruments add texture and never take the melody. The bed is the rhythm section underneath.

Tempo
100 bpm
Meter
4/4
Swing
straight
Key
E minor
Lead (carries the tune)
bright sawtooth synth
Colour (never the melody)
arpeggiator sparkle, gated toms
Bed
pulsing synth bass, drum machine, gated snare

Not this

Not chiptune or vaporwave: full analog-style synths and a steady pulse, no 8-bit bleeps, no slowed-down pop samples.

Other names

retrowave, 80s synth revival
