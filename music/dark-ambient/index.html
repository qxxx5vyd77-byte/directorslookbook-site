Suno Dark Ambient Prompt: Style and Exclude Lines

In the Lookbook this card is called Dark Ambient Horror.

Hear the style, 10 s: Dark Ambient

What it is

Low rumbling drones, detuned dissonant strings, metal scrapes and distant thuds. Nothing resolves; the quiet does the scaring.

Suno prompt

Two boxes, ready to paste. The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

dark ambient horror score, low sub rumble drone, dissonant tremolo and col legno strings, bowed metal scrapes, reversed piano notes, distant heartbeat thuds, cold dread, very slow, unresolved

Exclude

vocals, singing, choir, melody, upbeat, drum kit, trailer braam, EDM, rock, happy major key, lo-fi hip hop, orchestral fanfare

The parts

The lead is the one instrument that carries the tune. Colour instruments add texture and never take the melody. The bed is the rhythm section underneath.

Tempo
60 bpm
Meter
4/4
Swing
straight
Key
atonal, C# pedal
Lead (carries the tune)
none: texture and silence
Colour (never the melody)
bowed metal, reversed piano, string scrapes
Bed
sub rumble drone, heartbeat thuds

Not this

Not a jump-scare trailer: no braams or big hits. Not sad ambient: the dissonance stays and nothing resolves.

Other names

Dark Ambient Horror, horror drone, horror soundscape

Pairs with

  • slow creep down a dark hallway
  • underexposed cold green grade
  • a scratchy distressed serif title
