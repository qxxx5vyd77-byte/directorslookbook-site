Film Format Prompts: VHS, Disposable Camera, 2000s Digicam

Exact prompt wording for three consumer camera looks: VHS camcorder, disposable camera and 2000s digicam, with example frames.

  • VHSConsumer analog video from a shoulder camcorder: soft low-resolution image, colour smearing past edges, tracking noise bands, blown highlights, a date stamp.
  • Disposable CameraA single-use plastic camera: harsh on-camera flash, soft plastic-lens corners, a slightly off date-stamp era look.
  • 2000s DigicamAn early 2000s compact digital camera: small sensor, blown flash, cool cast, digital noise, slight purple fringing.
