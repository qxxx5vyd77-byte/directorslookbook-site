VHS Prompt: Exact Wording and Example Frame

In the Lookbook this card is called VHS Camcorder.

What it is

Consumer analog video from a shoulder camcorder: soft low-resolution image, colour smearing past edges, tracking noise bands, blown highlights, a date stamp.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

VHS camcorder footage, 1989 home video, soft low-resolution image, chroma bleed, tracking noise bands, blown-out highlights, on-screen date stamp in the corner, 4:3 frame

Not this

A sharp image with RGB-split glitch effects. VHS is soft everywhere, with colour smearing sideways.

Other names

VHS Camcorder, VHS tape, camcorder home video, analog video
