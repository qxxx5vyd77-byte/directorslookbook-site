Rim Light Prompt: Exact Wording and Example Frame

In the Lookbook this card is called Rim Light Kicker.

What it is

A light behind and to the side of the subject draws a bright line along hair, cheek and shoulder, lifting them off a dark background while the face stays mostly in shadow.

What it says: Separation, mystery, a figure carved from the night.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

rim light from behind and to one side, a thin bright edge along the hair, cheek and shoulder, subject separated from a dark background, face mostly in soft shadow, low-key

Not this

Not contre-jour into the sun and not a full silhouette: the source is a lamp, the background stays dark, and the face still reads.
