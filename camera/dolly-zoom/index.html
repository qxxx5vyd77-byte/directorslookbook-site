Dolly Zoom Prompt for AI Video: Exact Wording and Example

What it is

Track backward while zooming in, or the reverse. The subject holds the same size while the background stretches or collapses behind them.

What it says: Dread, the world lurching.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

dolly zoom, camera tracks backward while zooming in, subject stays the same size while the background stretches and looms behind

Not this

Not a plain zoom: the subject must hold size while the background warps.

Other names

contra-zoom, trombone shot
