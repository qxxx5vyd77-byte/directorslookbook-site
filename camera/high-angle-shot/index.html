High Angle Shot Prompt: Exact Wording and Example Frame

In the Lookbook this card is called High Angle.

What it is

The camera looks down on the subject from above, but not straight down. They read as small and exposed.

What it says: Vulnerability, being watched.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

high angle shot looking down on the subject from above, subject small and exposed in the frame

Not this

Not top-down: a high angle still shows a horizon or a far wall.
