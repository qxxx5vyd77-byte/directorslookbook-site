Problems the Lookbook fixes

The four problems we see most, in the words people use for them. Each one: what fixed looks like, the steps, how long it takes, and one result from our own videos.

What it costs: nothing, for all of them. No sign-up, no account.

01
PROBLEM 01

“My AI pictures all look the same. Everything comes out looking like AI.”

FIXED
The frame looks like one named film stock, lit one named way, through one named lens.
HOW
  1. Say what it is for (photo, video, song, site) and how it should feel.
  2. Deal a look: film stock, palette, light, lens.
  3. Swap any card that is wrong for your scene.
  4. Copy the words into your image or video tool.
HOW LONG
About a minute. Free.
ONE RESULT
For our music video Cyber Black October we picked CineStill 800T. It puts a red halo around every bright light, and that gave the whole film its red.
02
PROBLEM 02

“I know the look. I just don't know what it's called.”

FIXED
You have its name and the exact words that get it.
HOW
  1. Describe it in plain words in the search box.
  2. Open the closest card and look at the example.
  3. Read its “not” line to check it is this look and not the one next to it.
  4. Copy the words.
HOW LONG
Seconds. Free.
ONE RESULT
For a video about the internet of 2007 we wanted the blocky white caption font with a black outline. The library had no card for it, so we added one: Impact. When a name is missing, we add it.
03
PROBLEM 03

“I stacked five styles and the picture went flat.”

FIXED
Every part of the look does one job, and no two parts do the same job.
HOW
  1. Build the hand.
  2. Read the line under it: Cards agree, Cards disagree or Clash.
  3. On a clash, re-deal or drop the card that repeats another.
HOW LONG
Under a minute. Free.
ONE RESULT
For the second half of a French pop video we had picked a cold, desaturated grade on top of Fuji Classic Chrome. The check showed Classic Chrome already does that job. We dropped the grade.
04
PROBLEM 04

“Every shot in my video looks like the same shot.”

FIXED
Each beat has its own named shot and its own camera move.
HOW
  1. Pick the video job.
  2. Write down your beats: intro, build, drop, ending.
  3. Give each beat one camera card: a shot or a move.
  4. Paste each beat's words into your video tool.
HOW LONG
About five minutes for a short video. Free.
ONE RESULT
For our Halloween video: Fisheye for the mob, Worm's-Eye Low Angle for the squad, FPV Drone Dive for the build and Mid-Air Jump for the drop.
