Vintage Postage Stamp Poster

Also called: engraved travel stamp, commemorative stamp art, perforated stamp frame

What it is

A travel scene drawn as an old engraved postage stamp: fine hatching, aged cream paper, a perforated edge, a denomination corner and a titled banner, in muted ink colours.
