Chat Bubble Talk Deck

Also called: conference talk slides, chocolate ground deck, speech bubble slides, googly eye deck, scalloped wave slides, taped banner slides

What it is

Talk slides on a chocolate ground: flat violet, yellow, tangerine and hot pink panels, a tall flared Deco serif for titles, chat-bubble pills with tails as asides, scalloped wave edges, taped banner strips and a pair of googly eyes peeking in.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

talk slides on a dark chocolate ground, flat violet, yellow, tangerine and hot pink panels, tall flared Deco serif titles, chat-bubble pills with tails as asides, scalloped wave edge, taped banner strips, a pair of googly eyes, no photos, no gradients

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a corporate template: no white slides, no bullet lists, no drop shadows; the brown ground is the brand, each slide carries one big line, and the bubbles do the talking.
