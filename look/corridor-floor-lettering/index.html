Corridor Floor Lettering

Also called: anamorphic floor type, architectural word poster

What it is

A black-and-white photo down a long colonnade or arcade, one giant red word painted across floor and ceiling in forced perspective, a lone walker on the centre line.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

black and white architectural photo of a deep symmetrical corridor, giant red block letters painted across floor and ceiling in anamorphic perspective, one small figure walking the centre line, soft haze, film grain

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a type overlay: the letters sit on the architecture and recede with it, and red is the only colour.
