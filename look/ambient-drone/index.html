Ambient Drone

Also called: drone ambient, ambient soundscape

Hear the style, 10 s: Ambient Drone

What it is

Sustained tones that barely move: long evolving pads, bowed textures and tape haze. No beat and no tune, just a slowly shifting field of sound.

Suno prompt

The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

ambient drone, slowly evolving sustained analog pads, bowed string textures, soft tape-loop haze, low warm hum, distant swells, vast and weightless, beatless, very slow

Exclude

drums, beat, percussion, vocals, singing, humming, choir, piano melody, guitar, new age, crystal bowl, wind chimes, EDM, lo-fi hip hop, horror, dissonance

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not new-age spa music: no crystal bowls, wind chimes or gentle piano tune. Not horror: the harmony stays consonant.
