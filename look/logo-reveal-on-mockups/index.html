Logo Reveal on Mockups

Also called: brand identity reel, packaging mockup cut, negative-space logo film

What it is

A brand reveal cut: one negative-space mark in a two-colour palette, shown in quick beats on real-looking packaging (cups, bags, boxes) under hard studio light, ending on the mark alone on a flat field.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

brand identity reveal, a single negative-space logo mark in two colours applied to paper cups and packaging, hard studio light with crisp cast shadows, tactile paper texture, one flat colour field close, product photography

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a logo on a white canvas: the mark must sit on physical objects in real light, then end alone on its own colour.
