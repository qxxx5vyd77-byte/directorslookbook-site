Coloured Pencil

Also called: colored pencil drawing, pencil crayon, pencil drawing

What it is

Layered coloured pencil on paper: visible directional strokes, paper tooth showing through, soft burnished blends, gentle naturalistic colour.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

coloured pencil drawing, layered directional pencil strokes, white paper tooth showing through, soft burnished blends, gentle naturalistic colour, fine hatching in shadows, slightly waxy sheen, cream paper margins

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not wax crayon or digital airbrush. Strokes are fine and directional and the paper grain shows.
