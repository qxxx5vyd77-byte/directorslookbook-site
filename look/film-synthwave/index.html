Synthwave

Also called: outrun, retrowave, 80s neon grid

What it is

Retro-future 1980s night: a magenta-orange striped sun over a glowing laser grid, chrome, palm silhouettes, purple haze and a car on an endless road.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

synthwave outrun scene, huge striped sunset sun in magenta and orange, glowing cyan laser grid floor to the horizon, purple haze, palm tree silhouettes, sports car on an endless highway, chrome highlights, scanline glow

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not pastel vaporwave irony or rain-slick cyberpunk. Synthwave is a neon sunset and a road.
