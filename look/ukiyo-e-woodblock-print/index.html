Ukiyo-e Woodblock Print

Also called: ukiyo-e, Japanese woodblock print, Edo woodblock

What it is

Edo-period woodblock prints that move: flat colour blocks, a carved black key line, bokashi skies and visible washi paper, each colour a little off register.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

ukiyo-e woodblock print, sumi key-block line, flat Prussian blue and pale blue with a bokashi gradient sky, beni red sun, washi paper fibres, woodgrain in flat colour, slight misregistration

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not flat vector with a paper overlay, not Chinese ink wash, not anime cel shading with light and shadow on forms.

The details

Era
1670 to 1900, Edo and early Meiji Japan
Type that suits it
Shippori Mincho
Camera native to it
handscroll slide, right to left, locked frame on one sheet, hard cut to a blank sheet
Sound
shamisen, shakuhachi, koto; wooden clappers, baren rub, silence held like a breath
