Studio Softbox Portrait

Also called: studio lighting, three-point lighting, paper sweep backdrop

What it is

Controlled studio light: a big soft key at 45 degrees, fill to open the shadows, a rim light to separate the subject from a plain paper backdrop.

What it says: Full attention on one subject.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

studio portrait lighting, large octabox key light at 45 degrees, soft fill, thin rim light separating the subject, plain grey paper sweep backdrop, catchlights in the eyes, controlled soft shadows

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not hard top light or on-camera flash. The light wraps, the background is controlled and plain.
