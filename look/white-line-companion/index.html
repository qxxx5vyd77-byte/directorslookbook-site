White Line Companion

Also called: glowing line character in a photo, chalk-line friend, one-line creature over a real scene

What it is

A real, warmly lit photograph of a cozy corner (window seat, attic, garden wall) with one small animal drawn into it as a single glowing white line, as if sketched in light.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

a real warmly lit photograph of a cozy interior corner at dusk, with one small animal drawn over it as a single glowing white outline, soft glow, no fill, the photo kept realistic, 3:4

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a cartoon composite: the setting is photographic and the creature is only a line of light.
