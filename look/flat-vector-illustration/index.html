Flat Vector Illustration

Also called: vector art, flat illustration, editorial vector

What it is

Shape-based digital illustration: solid flat fills, no outlines or thin ones, simple geometric characters, limited palette, subtle grain or gradient shading.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

flat vector illustration, solid flat colour shapes, simple geometric characters with no facial detail, limited five-colour palette of teal, coral and mustard, soft grain shading, crisp edges, balanced editorial composition

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a corporate stock doodle with tiny heads and huge limbs. Keep strong shapes and a tight palette.
