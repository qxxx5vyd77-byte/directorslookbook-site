Worm's-Eye Low Angle

Also called: low angle, hero angle

What it is

The camera sits near the ground and looks up. The subject towers against the sky.

What it says: Power, monument, the hero towers.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

low angle shot from near ground level looking up at the subject, towering over the camera against the sky, wide lens

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a dutch angle: the horizon stays level, only the eye line drops.
