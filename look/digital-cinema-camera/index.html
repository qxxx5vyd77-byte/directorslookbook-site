Digital Cinema Camera

Also called: cinema camera look, 6K cinema sensor, log-graded video

What it is

A large-sensor digital cinema camera: huge dynamic range, soft highlight roll-off, clean shadows, cinema-lens shallow focus, colour set in the grade rather than baked in. The look of modern film and prestige streaming.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

shot on a large-sensor digital cinema camera with cinema prime lenses, very wide dynamic range, soft highlight roll-off, noise-free shadows, shallow cinematic focus, graded log footage, 2.39:1 frame

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not video-camera sharpness and not film grain: smooth, deep and graded. Name the lens look and the grade, not a camera brand.
