Plinth Still Life, Red Accent

Also called: surreal product still life, plinth still life, art-directed still life, sculptural product photography, red accent still life, product on a plinth

What it is

The art-directed product still life: one sculptural object on a chalk-white plinth in a cool powder-blue studio, lit by a single hard flash that throws one crisp shadow. One small red thing is the only warm colour in frame. A straight-on normal lens, lots of empty wall and fine film grain keep it an editorial print, not a catalogue packshot.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

editorial still life photograph, one sculptural object on a chalk-white plinth against a cool powder-blue studio wall, a single small red accent, hard direct flash casting one crisp dark shadow, wide negative space, straight-on 50mm lens, fine film grain

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a white-sweep packshot: the wall is a cool colour, the light is one hard flash, and the red accent is placed on purpose. Not a glossy 3D render: real grain and a real cast shadow. One object and one red accent, never a crowded tabletop.
