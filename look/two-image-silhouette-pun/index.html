Two-Image Silhouette Pun

Also called: negative-space visual pun, figure inside a figure, two-colour double read

What it is

Two images in one silhouette: a black animal whose chest is a second white animal, no outline, two flat colours, the joke lands when the eye flips between them.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

two-colour negative-space visual pun, one silhouette containing a second animal in the counter shape, flat black and white, no outlines, no grey, poster composition

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a plain negative-space figure: the second image must read as its own subject, and the pun is the whole point.
