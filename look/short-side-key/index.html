Short-Side Key

Also called: short lighting, shoot the shadow side, negative fill, camera on the dark side of the face

What it is

The key light falls on the side of the face turned away from the camera, so the lens looks into the shadow side. A black flag on the near side removes bounced light, so the near cheek stays dark and the face has depth.

What it says: A person turned inward, holding something back.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

short lighting, soft key 45 degrees off axis on the far side of the face, slightly above eye level, camera on the shadow side, negative fill from a black flag keeping the near cheek dark, gentle falloff, room darker behind

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not broad lighting, where the lit side faces the lens and the face looks flat and wide. Not split light: the far side is fully lit and the near side only falls off, it does not go black.
