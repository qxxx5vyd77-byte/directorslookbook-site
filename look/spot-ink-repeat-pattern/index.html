Spot-Ink Repeat Pattern

Also called: riso repeat pattern, spot ink textile print, all-over animal pattern, wrapping paper pattern, three-ink surface pattern

What it is

A surface pattern rather than a picture: one motif, usually an animal, repeated edge to edge at different angles until it fills the frame. Three flat spot inks do all the work, hot pink, cream and cobalt, with a hairline green for fur or feather strokes. The fills carry a dry stencil texture and the inks sit slightly off register.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

all-over repeat pattern, one animal motif repeated edge to edge at varied angles, three flat spot inks, hot pink, cream and cobalt, with a hairline green, textured stencil fills, no gradients, no ground or horizon, wrapping paper or textile print

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a scene: no ground, no sky, no horizon, no single hero animal. No gradients, no shading, no outlines in black. The motif repeats; it does not pose. Four flat colours at most.
