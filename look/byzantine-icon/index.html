Byzantine Icon

Also called: Orthodox icon, egg tempera icon, gold ground panel

What it is

Egg tempera on wood under burnished gold leaf: frontal still figures, almond eyes, flat haloes, stylised drapery lines and reverse perspective.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

Byzantine icon style, egg tempera on wooden panel, burnished gold leaf background, frontal solemn figure with flat halo, stylised drapery highlight lines, deep red and lapis blue, reverse perspective, aged craquelure

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a stained glass window or Renaissance realism. Figures are flat, frontal and set on gold.
