Technicolor Three-Strip

Also called: three-strip colour, dye transfer print, 1940s studio colour

What it is

Three-negative colour process of the 1930s to 50s, printed by dye transfer: lush saturated primaries, ruby reds, emerald greens, glowing skin, painted skies.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

three-strip Technicolor dye transfer print, 1940s studio musical, saturated ruby red and emerald green, glowing skin tones, hard key light, painted backdrop sky, rich deep shadows

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Digital colourization or neon saturation. Three-strip colour is dense and painterly, lit hard on a studio set.
