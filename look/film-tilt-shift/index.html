Tilt-Shift Miniature

Also called: fake miniature, miniature faking, toy town time-lapse

What it is

A real place seen from high above through a tilted lens: one thin sharp band, saturated sunny colour and stuttering time-lapse make the world read as a tabletop model.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

tilt-shift miniature effect, high oblique aerial view, thin horizontal band of sharp focus with heavy blur above and below, saturated sunny model-railway colours, hard sun with blue shadows, time-lapse traffic

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a toy or clay render, not a game's top-down view, not plain drone footage: the band, saturation and stutter are all required.
