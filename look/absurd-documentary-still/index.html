Absurd Documentary Still

Also called: viral candid still, deadpan photojournalism, unusual subject, real place

What it is

A straight-faced news photo of something that should not be there: an unlikely subject doing an ordinary chore in a worn, crowded real place, shot handheld with long-lens flaws while nobody nearby reacts.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

candid documentary photograph, handheld and slightly tilted, an unlikely subject doing an ordinary task in a worn, crowded real place, bystanders not reacting, telephoto compression, sensor noise, mild oversharpening, foreground occlusion

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a staged comedy shoot or a clean stock photo: the place is used and messy, the subject is unaware of the camera, and the picture has real camera flaws.
