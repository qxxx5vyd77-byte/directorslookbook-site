Whiteboard Explainer

Also called: whiteboard animation, dry-erase explainer, doodle explainer

What it is

An explainer drawn live on one glossy dry-erase board: handwriting written stroke by stroke, floating markers with no hand, magnets, a camera that travels and never cuts.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

whiteboard animation, glossy dry-erase board, hand-drawn black, blue and orange marker lines with streaky dry ink, single-line handwriting, faint ghost marks of erased lessons, soft window sheen, no hands

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a blue blueprint, not a crayon picture book, not kinetic type: text is written stroke by stroke, never animated in blocks.
