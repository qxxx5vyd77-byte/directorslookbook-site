Mezzotint

Also called: mezzotint print, rocked plate intaglio, velvet black print

What it is

Intaglio print in which the whole plate is roughened to print velvet black and the image is scraped and burnished out of the dark: tone without line, deep blacks, soft greys, small sharp highlights.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

mezzotint print, image scraped out of a fully rocked velvet-black plate, tones emerging from darkness into soft grey and small bright highlights, no outlines, grainy burr texture, monochrome on cream paper

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not line engraving or etching with hatched lines. Tone only, dark to light, with a velvety grain; no printed titles or lettering under the image.

The details

Era
1642 onward, Netherlands and England, then worldwide
Type that suits it
Bodoni Moda, Pinyon Script
Camera native to it
slow reveal out of black, locked frontal on the sheet, macro on the burr grain
Sound
solo viola, wind, a press turning, paper lifted from the plate
