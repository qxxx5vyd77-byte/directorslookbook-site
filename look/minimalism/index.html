Minimalism

Also called: Minimal art, ABC art, primary structures

What it is

1960s art of the plain object: identical boxes and stripes in a row, industrial materials, one colour or none, no composition, no gesture, gallery light does the rest.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

1960s Minimalist art, a row of identical rectangular boxes in brushed aluminium and one flat industrial colour, white gallery wall, even flat lighting, exact repetition, no gesture, no ornament, wide empty floor

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not Scandinavian interior styling and not flat graphic design. Sculpture-first: plain repeated industrial objects, no composition.
