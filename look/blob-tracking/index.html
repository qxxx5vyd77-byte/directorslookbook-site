Blob Tracking

Also called: computer vision tracking, tracking boxes overlay, motion tracking HUD

What it is

Ordinary footage watched by a machine: thin white boxes snap onto moving points, tiny coordinate labels and ID numbers tick, and hairlines join the boxes as the subject moves.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

computer-vision blob tracking overlay on footage: thin white rectangular bounding boxes on moving points, tiny monospace coordinates and ID numbers, thin lines joining boxes, a few inverted patches inside boxes, cool desaturated footage

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a sci-fi HUD: no glowing interface, no circles or targets. Thin plain boxes and numbers only, over real footage.
