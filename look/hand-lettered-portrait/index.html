Hand-Lettered Portrait

Also called: brush lettering over a photo, painted type portrait, lettered portrait poster

What it is

A real photo portrait with big multicolour brush lettering painted over and around the face: rough dry-brush capitals, paint spatter, flat colour blocks behind the head.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

photo portrait with huge brush-painted lettering scrawled over and around the face, rough dry-brush capitals in clashing colours, paint spatter and drips, flat colour blocks behind the head

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a tidy caption on a photo: the letters are painted by hand, cross the face and the frame, and the photo stays real underneath.
