Flamenco

Also called: Andalusian flamenco, flamenco guitar

Hear the style, 10 s: Flamenco

What it is

Andalusian song, guitar and dance: fierce strummed guitar and fast runs over a twelve-count rhythm, hand claps and foot stamps, with a raw voice that bends between notes.

Suno prompt

The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

flamenco, nylon-string flamenco guitar with rasgueado strums and fast picado runs, sharp palmas hand claps, cajon, foot stamps, raw passionate male cante vocal, Phrygian harmony, 3/4 compas around 120 bpm

Exclude

reggaeton beat, pop drums, synth, EDM, electric guitar, orchestral strings, smooth jazz, Spanish pop ballad, lo-fi

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not Spanish pop guitar: raw and rhythmic, never soft and strummy. Not mariachi: no trumpets, no violins.
