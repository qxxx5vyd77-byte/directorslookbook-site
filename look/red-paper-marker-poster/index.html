Red Paper Marker Poster

Also called: two-ink community poster, red paper black marker, blunt marker people, fat marker poster, red ground figure poster

What it is

A community event poster where the paper is the colour: a full-bleed field of warm vermilion and one fat black marker doing everything else. People with big irregular round heads, dot eyes, a ring nose and mitten hands, their bodies left the same red as the ground inside a black outline, flat and unshaded. One shared object makes the scene. A bold grotesk headline, a short italic serif line, a small info line, nothing else.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

community event poster, full bleed warm vermilion red paper, two inks only, people in broad blunt wobbly black marker, big round heads, dot eyes, ring noses, mitten hands, bodies the same red as the ground, flat, no shading, dots on one garment, calm red space

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a painted red field and not a caricature: the figures are blunt marker outlines, round and friendly, never exaggerated. No white fills, no cream paper, no gradients, no 3D, no logos, no third colour. Pattern lives on one or two garments only; most of the red stays empty.
