Art Nouveau

Also called: whiplash curve, Belle Epoque poster, 1890s decorative poster

What it is

1890s to 1910s decorative style: sinuous whiplash curves, botanical ornament, figures framed by halos and arches, flat muted colour inside flowing outlines.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

Art Nouveau poster, sinuous whiplash curves, botanical ornament border, flowing keyline outlines, flat muted sage, gold and dusty rose, circular halo behind the figure, decorative lettering

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Art Deco. Nouveau is curved and botanical, all flowing line; Deco is geometric, stepped and metallic.
