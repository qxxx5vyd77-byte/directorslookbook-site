Six-Panel Street Editorial

Also called: streetwear lookbook grid, 3x2 editorial contact sheet, playful city campaign grid

What it is

A 3x2 grid of one person in one outfit across six camera positions: a low wide, a tight smile, a jump, a top-down on a zebra crossing, a shoe close-up, a rooftop wide, all in hard sun on city streets.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

six-panel editorial grid, one person in one outfit, six camera positions (low wide, close smile, mid-jump, top-down crossing, shoe close-up, rooftop wide), hard sunlight, city streets, saturated accent colour

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not six random photos: same outfit, same light, one accent colour, each panel a different camera position.
