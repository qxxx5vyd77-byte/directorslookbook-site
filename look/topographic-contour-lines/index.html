Topographic Contour Lines

Also called: contour-map figures, elevation line drawing, terrain line portrait

What it is

A face, hand or landscape built only from closed contour lines, as if it were a hill mapped in elevation: lines bunch where the form is steep and spread where it is flat, one ink colour on a plain ground.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

topographic contour line illustration, a face built only from closed elevation lines that bunch on steep forms and spread on flat ones, single dark ink on cream, no shading or fill, even line weight, frontal

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a wireframe mesh (no crossing lines) and not a one-line drawing: every line is a closed loop that never crosses another.
