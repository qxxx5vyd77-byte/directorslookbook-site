Gospel Choir

Also called: church gospel, gospel praise

Hear the style, 10 s: Gospel Choir

What it is

African American church music: a full choir answering a lead singer, organ and piano trading chords, tambourine and hand claps, swaying in a 12/8 feel.

Suno prompt

The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

gospel choir, traditional church style, full choir call and response with a soaring lead vocal, gospel organ, rolling piano, tambourine and hand claps, joyful and rising, swaying 12/8 feel, 70 bpm

Exclude

rap, autotune, trap, EDM, plainchant, cathedral choir, opera, rock guitar, synth pad, lo-fi, children's choir

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a cathedral choir or plainchant: this is call and response with organ, piano and hand claps, raw and joyful.
