Cyanotype Sun Print

Also called: cyanotype, sun print, photogram

What it is

Iron-salt photographic print exposed in sunlight: a Prussian blue ground with pale silhouettes wherever objects or a negative blocked the light.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

cyanotype sun print, Prussian blue ground, white botanical silhouettes, photogram of pressed ferns and leaves, brushed emulsion edges, watercolour paper texture

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

A blueprint drawing. A cyanotype is a photographic print: soft silhouettes and brushed edges, not ruled technical lines.
