Spectral Grain Figures

Also called: rainbow gradient figures, grainy spectrum illustration, prism-fill characters

What it is

Simple flat figures filled with soft rainbow gradients that drift from orange to green to blue inside each shape, dusted with a fine stipple grain, on an off-white paper ground. No outlines; the colour does the drawing.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

flat illustrated figures with no outlines, each shape filled with a soft spectral gradient blending orange, green and blue, fine stippled grain texture, off-white paper ground, simple gestures, lots of empty space

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a holographic foil and not a risograph two-ink print: the gradient flows through every colour inside one shape, and the grain is fine and even.
