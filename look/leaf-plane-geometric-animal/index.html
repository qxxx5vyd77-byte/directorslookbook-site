Leaf-Plane Geometric Animal

Also called: deer from leaf shapes, symmetrical flat-plane creature, olive and gold geometric nature

What it is

An animal composed entirely of leaf-shaped flat planes, olive and gold, mirrored into near symmetry, a decorative emblem more than a creature, on a quiet ground.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

ultra-geometric nature illustration, deer composed of leaf-shaped flat planes, olive and gold, near-symmetrical, no outlines, decorative emblem composition, quiet cream ground

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not low-poly 3D and not a logo: the planes are leaves with a hand-painted edge and the animal stays an illustration.
