Linocut Print

Also called: lino relief print, bold curved-cut print, reduction linocut, linocut, lino print, lino cut

What it is

Relief print from soft lino: cuts curve freely where wood would splinter, so forms are bolder and rounder, two or three flat colours printed in layers with slight misregistration, broad confident whites.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

linocut print, bold flat shapes with curved gouge cuts, two-colour reduction print of indigo and ochre on cream paper, slight misregistration, broad confident white cuts, visible ink roller texture, coastal scene

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a woodcut (no grain, no splinter) and not a vector poster: the cuts are curved and the ink is uneven.
