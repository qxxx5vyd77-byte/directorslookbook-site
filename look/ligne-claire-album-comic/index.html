Ligne Claire Album Comic

Also called: clear-line comic, Franco-Belgian album style, even-weight colour comic

What it is

The album comic style: one thin even black line for everything, no hatching, flat colour fills with no shadow, backgrounds drawn as carefully as faces, every element equally in focus, panels in bright daylight.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

ligne claire comic illustration, thin even black line with no variation, no hatching, flat colour fills without cast shadows, fully drawn background in equal detail, bright daylight, one wide panel, printed album page

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not manga and not an American comic: no screentone, no brush line, no dramatic ink shadow.
