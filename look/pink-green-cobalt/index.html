Pink Green Cobalt

Also called: three-hue poster palette, hot pink emerald cobalt

What it is

Hot pink, emerald and cobalt on warm cream with black accents: three hues that never mix, each owning whole shapes.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

three-colour poster palette: hot pink, emerald green and cobalt blue, each filling whole shapes, black accents, warm cream background, no other hues

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.
