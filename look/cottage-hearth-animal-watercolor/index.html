Cottage Hearth Animal Watercolor

Also called: dressed animal watercolor, lamplit cottage animals, miniature hearth interior

What it is

Fine transparent watercolor with coloured-pencil detail on warm paper: small animals in cardigans and dresses inside a lamplit cottage crammed with homely things.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

fine transparent watercolor with coloured-pencil detailing on warm paper, small animals in cardigans and dresses, snug lamplit cottage interior, gingham armchair, hearth with a hanging kettle, golden lamplight, cranberry and soft blue

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not the looser moss and rust storybook wash, and not opaque gouache: fine detail, transparent glazes, a crowded domestic interior.
