Chromatic Fringe

Also called: RGB edge split, prism fringing, lens colour separation

What it is

Colour separation as the look: red and blue edges pulling apart from every contour as if seen through a cheap prism, strongest at the frame edges, the centre nearly whole, otherwise a normal photograph.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

chromatic aberration photograph, red and cyan fringes separating from every edge, strongest toward the frame corners and almost none at centre, backlit subject, otherwise natural colour and sharpness, slight lens flare

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not glitch art (no blocks, no tearing) and not a 3D anaglyph: it is a lens fault, soft and radial.
