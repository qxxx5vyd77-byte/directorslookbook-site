Periwinkle, Lemon and Rust

Also called: unexpected pairing palette, periwinkle and lemon, citrus print palette

What it is

A periwinkle blue ground with pale lemon yellow and a burnt rust accent: cool and sour against warm and earthy, the clash that makes it sing.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

flat illustration colour palette: periwinkle blue ground, pale lemon yellow forms and a burnt rust orange accent for outlines and small details, matte, halftone friendly

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not sky blue and primary yellow: the blue leans lavender, the yellow is pale and sour, and rust stays a small accent.
