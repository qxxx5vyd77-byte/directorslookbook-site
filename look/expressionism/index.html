Expressionism

Also called: German Expressionism, expressionist painting, angst painting

What it is

Early 1900s painting of inner feeling: jagged distorted shapes, acid greens and blood reds, harsh black contours, tilted streets and mask-like faces.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

German Expressionist painting, jagged distorted forms, harsh black outlines, acid green faces and blood-red sky, tilted angular buildings, raw slashing brushstrokes, unsettling perspective, rough unprimed canvas

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a horror film still or a messy filter. Distortion is deliberate and carries emotion.
