One-Point Perspective

Also called: vanishing point symmetry, corridor shot, central perspective, symmetrical hallway shot

What it is

The camera looks straight down a corridor or road so every line runs to one vanishing point in the exact centre of the frame, with the subject small on that point.

What it says: Control, dread, a place that is watching.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

one-point perspective shot, camera centred and level looking straight down a long corridor, all lines converging on a single vanishing point in the exact centre, perfect symmetry left and right, subject small on the vanishing point, wide lens, deep focus

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not Planimetric Tableau: the space rushes away in depth to one point, it does not sit flat against a back wall.
