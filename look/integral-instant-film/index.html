Integral Instant Film

Also called: instant photo, instant print, square instant film

What it is

A self-developing integral print: square image in a white frame with a deeper bottom border, soft focus, creamy faded colour and a slight colour cast.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

integral instant film print, square image in a white frame with a deep bottom border, soft focus, low contrast creamy faded colour, slight cyan-green cast

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

A phone photo dropped into a frame template. Instant film is soft, flash-lit and low in contrast, with uneven chemistry.
