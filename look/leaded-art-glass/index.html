Leaded Art Glass

Also called: Art Nouveau glass and metal, opalescent lamp glass, whiplash ironwork

What it is

Art Nouveau glass and metalwork, 1890 to 1915: streaky opalescent glass cut and set in copper foil and lead, lamp shades of dragonflies and wisteria lit from inside, wrought iron that curls like a stem, bronze patina.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

Art Nouveau leaded glass, opalescent and streaky glass pieces set in copper foil and dark lead lines, dragonfly and wisteria lamp shade glowing from within, whiplash wrought iron curves, bronze patina, warm amber, violet and leaf green

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not Gothic cathedral glass with painted faces and not Art Deco geometry. Organic curves, mottled opalescent glass, metal that grows like a stem.
