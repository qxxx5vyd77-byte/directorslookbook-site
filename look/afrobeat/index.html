Afrobeat

Also called: Afro-funk, 1970s Lagos funk

Hear the style, 10 s: Afrobeat

What it is

1970s West African fusion of highlife, funk and jazz: long hypnotic grooves of interlocking guitars, a unison horn section, busy drums and shekere.

Suno prompt

The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

1970s Nigerian Afrobeat instrumental, interlocking choppy funk guitars, punchy horn section in unison, busy syncopated drums with shekere and congas, rolling bass ostinato, electric organ, hypnotic and defiant, 112 bpm

Exclude

vocals, singing, rap, Afrobeats pop, dancehall, reggaeton, EDM, trap hi-hats, autotune, lo-fi, orchestral, synth pad

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not Afrobeats: that 2010s pop style has sung hooks and a lighter bounce. This is long, live and horn-driven.
