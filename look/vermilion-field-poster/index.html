Vermilion Field Poster

Also called: red field poster, grey figure on red, charcoal figure poster, italic serif editorial poster, red and grey art poster

What it is

A flat field of saturated vermilion with the subject painted in cool greys, near-black and chalk white, in angular dry brush strokes. A huge sharp-cut italic serif title sits behind parts of the subject, with a thin white outline copy crossing the foreground, plus one small engineering sketch and a tiny caption.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

editorial art poster, flat vermilion field, subject painted in cool greys, near-black and chalk white, dry charcoal strokes, hard raking light, bold cropping, huge italic serif title behind the subject with a thin outline copy in front, grain on the red

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a glossy render and not a photo: the subject is painted, the red is flat, and there is no scenery behind it. No neon, no gradients, no logos. The lettering is part of the look: the title and caption carry invented words.
