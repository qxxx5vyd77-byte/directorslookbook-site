Bean-Mouth Noodle 3D

Also called: streaming-era rounded 3D, noodle-limb characters, bean mouth style

What it is

Streaming-era 3D character design: round heads, oval eyes, a small bean-shaped mouth floating on the face, noodle arms with no elbows, soft matte shading and a candy-neutral palette.
