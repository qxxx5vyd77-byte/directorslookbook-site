Flat City Wallpaper

Also called: phone wallpaper cityscape, lock-screen illustration, dense flat town under big clouds

What it is

A tall phone-wallpaper illustration: a dense flat-colour town or city of brick orange, teal and cream climbs the frame under towering cumulus in a saturated blue sky, with one small vehicle for scale and a big empty sky band for the clock.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

tall 9:19.5 phone wallpaper illustration, dense flat-colour city of brick orange, teal and cream, towering cumulus clouds in a saturated blue sky, one small vehicle for scale, clear sky band at the top for a clock, crisp print texture

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a landscape photo: colours are flat and limited, and the top third stays open for the lock-screen clock.
