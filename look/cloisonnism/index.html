Cloisonnism

Also called: cloisonné painting, dark-contour flat colour, enamel-cell canvas

What it is

Late-1880s painting that borrows the enamel-cell look: flat areas of unmixed colour fenced by heavy dark contours, no modelling, simplified rural figures, a tilted ground plane, like stained glass painted in oil.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

cloisonnist oil painting, flat unmixed colour areas bounded by heavy dark blue contours, no modelling or shading, simplified rural figures in headscarves, tilted green field, vermilion and chrome yellow accents, matte canvas

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not stained glass (it is paint on canvas) and not Fauvism (the colour stays local, the contour does the work).
