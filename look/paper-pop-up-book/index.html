Paper Pop-up Book

Also called: pop-up book, paper diorama, cut-paper stage

What it is

A pop-up book on a real desk becomes a stage: flat cut-paper actors with white borders act in front of paper sets that spring up, under real lamp light and a macro lens.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

pop-up book diorama on a wooden desk, flat cut-paper characters with thick off-white borders and dark brown ink outlines, V-fold paper scenery rising from the page, warm lamp light, macro lens shallow focus, cut-out shadows

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a backlit paper-cut silhouette, not a rounded 3D cartoon, not claymation: paper hinges, folds and slides, it never squishes.
