Renaissance Oil Painting

Also called: High Renaissance, Italian Renaissance painting, old master oil

What it is

Oil on panel from the 1400s and 1500s, born in Flanders and carried to Italy: calm pyramidal figure groups, soft sfumato modelling, linear perspective, blue-green distant hills and warm glazed flesh.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

Renaissance oil painting on wood panel, soft sfumato modelling, pyramidal figure composition, one-point linear perspective, hazy blue atmospheric hills, warm translucent glazes, ultramarine and vermilion drapery

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not dark tenebrist drama and not a glossy fantasy render. Light is even, calm and gently modelled.
