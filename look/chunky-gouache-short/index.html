Chunky Gouache Short

Also called: digital gouache animated short, soft chunky brush storybook, pink-sky gouache animation

What it is

A contemporary animated short painted in digital gouache: chunky simplified shapes, soft brush texture, pink and lilac skies, grey-blue rooftops, bright accent colours and no outlines.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Not this

Not vintage dry-brush gouache with primaries and not watercolour: the paint is opaque, soft and contemporary.
