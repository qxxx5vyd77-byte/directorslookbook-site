Psychedelic Poster

Also called: 1960s concert poster, acid poster, op-psych

What it is

1960s concert-poster art: warped melting shapes, vibrating complementary colours, swirling Art Nouveau curves, paisley and radiating patterns.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

1960s psychedelic poster art, melting warped organic shapes, clashing complementary colours (magenta on green, orange on blue), swirling Art Nouveau curves, paisley, radiating concentric patterns, flat screen-printed inks

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not neon synthwave or a kaleidoscope filter. Shapes melt and swirl, colours fight on purpose.
