Roman Inscription Caps

Also called: inscriptional capitals, carved Roman caps, epic title caps

THE NINTH WINTER

What it is

Capitals only, proportioned like letters chiseled into first-century Roman stone: round O, narrow E and S, small wedge serifs. The stock voice of a historical epic.

The prompt

Paste it into an image prompt that renders text, or use the font itself for titles.

Prompt

Roman inscriptional capitals chiseled into marble, V-cut strokes, classical proportions, small wedge serifs, all caps with wide letter spacing, gold leaf on dark stone, historical title card

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a fashion Didone: contrast is gentle and the serifs are wedges, not hairlines. No lowercase, ever.
