Giant-Glyph Exhibition Poster

Also called: East Asian exhibition poster, big Chinese character typography poster, vertical and horizontal CJK type poster, Korean Japanese Chinese graphic design poster

What it is

A contemporary East Asian exhibition poster: giant Chinese, Japanese or Korean characters run both down and across a saturated flat ground, one cut-paper shape sits in the middle, and tiny columns of details fill the gaps.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

contemporary East Asian exhibition poster, giant heavy CJK characters set both vertically and horizontally, saturated flat colour ground, one flat cut-paper shape in the centre, tiny columns of detail text in the gaps, three flat inks

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a brush calligraphy scroll and not a quiet Swiss grid: the characters are heavy print type used as the picture, crowding the shape.
