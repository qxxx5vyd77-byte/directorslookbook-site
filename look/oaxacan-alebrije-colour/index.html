Oaxacan Alebrije Colour

Also called: alebrijes, Oaxacan wood carving, Mexican fantastical folk carving

What it is

Mexican folk carving of imagined creatures: copal wood or papier-mache painted in saturated acrylic with dense dot, stripe and scallop patterns, every surface covered.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

Mexican alebrije folk carving style, imagined hybrid creature in painted copal wood, saturated magenta, cobalt, lime and orange, dense dot, stripe and scallop patterning covering every surface, fine brush details, matte acrylic

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a cartoon monster or Day of the Dead skulls. Do not copy a carver family's motifs or call it pre-Hispanic; a 20th century living craft.
