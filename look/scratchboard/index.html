Scratchboard

Also called: scraperboard, white-line clay board, scratched engraving look

What it is

A board coated black over white clay: the image is scratched out with a knife, so every mark is a white line on black, fur and feathers rendered stroke by stroke, with an optional transparent ink tint over the whites.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

scratchboard illustration, white lines scratched from a black clay board, fine parallel strokes following fur and feather direction, dense black in shadow, a few scratched highlights, one transparent amber ink tint, animal portrait

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a woodcut (strokes are needle-fine) and not inverted line art: the knife builds tone by stroke density.
