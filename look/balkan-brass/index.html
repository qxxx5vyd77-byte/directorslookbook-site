Balkan Brass

Also called: Balkan brass band, brass wedding band

Hear the style, 10 s: Balkan Brass

What it is

Southeastern European brass band music: trumpets and flugelhorns blasting fast ornamented lines in unison, tuba pumping the bass and a big drum driving a wedding crowd.

Suno prompt

The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

Balkan brass band, 140 bpm, wild trumpet and flugelhorn unison lines, tuba oom-pah bass, snare and big bass drum, fast ornamented runs, harmonic minor, wedding-party frenzy, instrumental

Exclude

vocals, electronic beat, synth, guitar, EDM drop, slow ballad, big band swing, mariachi, orchestra

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a marching band: wild, ornamented and swaying, not drilled. Not mariachi: no violins, no guitars.
