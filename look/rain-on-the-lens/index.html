Rain on the Lens

Also called: wet lens, drops on the glass, rain-streaked lens

What it is

Water on the front element: drops and streaks on the glass, lights blooming through them, the scene seen through weather.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

with rain on the lens: water drops and streaks on the front of the lens, lights blooming and refracting through the drops, the scene softened behind the wet glass

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not rain in the scene: the water is on the camera.
