Arc-and-Halo Geometric Editorial

Also called: sacred geometry vector illustration, Bauhaus sacred geometry style, navy terracotta gold geometric illustration, compass-drawn editorial illustration

What it is

A news or story scene rebuilt from compass arcs: faceless figures and buildings cut into flat navy, terracotta and cream shapes, with thin gold circles, halos and rays drawn behind them like an emblem.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

flat geometric vector illustration built from compass arcs and circles, faceless figures and buildings as flat shapes in deep navy, terracotta and cream, thin gold concentric circles, halo and ray lines behind the subject, symmetrical emblem layout

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not Bauhaus primaries and not Art Deco ornament: the palette is navy, terracotta, cream and gold, and the gold lines are plain compass circles, not decoration.
