Pattern-Wrapped Hyperreal

Also called: ornamental hyperrealism, decorative-pattern portrait, hyperreal face in ornament

What it is

A photoreal face or hand rendered with hyperreal care, then wrapped or veiled in dense decorative pattern: damask, paisley, lace or tile motifs that follow the form and sometimes replace the skin.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

hyperrealistic portrait wrapped in dense decorative pattern, damask and paisley motifs following the skin and merging with the background, muted gold and indigo, soft studio light, photoreal eyes and lashes, close-up

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a flat pattern overlay: the ornament bends over the form, catches the same light and stops at the eyes.
