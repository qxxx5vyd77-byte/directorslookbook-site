Cozy Celtic Folk

Also called: cottagecore folk, Celtic folk instrumental, pastoral folk

Hear the style, 10 s: Cozy Celtic Folk

What it is

Gentle Irish folk for cooking or study: a keyboard or guitar states the tune while Celtic harp and tin whistle sit underneath. Flat on purpose, nothing builds.

Suno prompt

The Style line goes in Suno's Styles field, the Exclude line in Exclude styles.

Style

gentle Irish Celtic folk instrumental, cozy pastoral cottagecore, felt piano leads the melody, Celtic harp and tin whistle only as colour, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft cello, unhurried, 72 bpm

Exclude

vocals, singing, humming, wordless vocals, choir, crescendo, swell, epic build, cinematic trailer, driving drum kit, jig, reel, uptempo dance, new age, shimmer pad wash, synth pad, 80s synth, vinyl crackle, lo-fi, field recording, music box, glockenspiel

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a session tune: once harp or whistle carries the melody it reads too strong. Not a lullaby: no music box, no sleep tags.
