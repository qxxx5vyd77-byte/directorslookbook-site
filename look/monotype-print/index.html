Monotype Print

Also called: monoprint, monotype, single-pull print

What it is

A one-off print pulled from ink painted or wiped on a smooth plate: soft clouded tones, wiped-out highlights, brushy edges and a faint plate mark. Only one strong impression exists.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

monotype print, ink rolled and wiped on a smooth plate, soft clouded tones, highlights wiped out with a rag, brushy edges, faint plate mark, one-off impression on cream paper

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a woodcut or an etching: no carved or bitten lines, the image is tone, not line.
