Islamic Geometric Ornament

Also called: girih pattern, arabesque tilework, muqarnas and zellij

What it is

Ornament of the Islamic world from the 800s on: girih star-and-polygon lattices, arabesque tendrils, glazed tile mosaic and muqarnas honeycomb vaults, pattern that fills a surface without a centre.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

Islamic geometric ornament, interlocking girih star and polygon lattice, arabesque tendrils, glazed tile mosaic in cobalt, turquoise, white and ochre, muqarnas honeycomb vault above, flat symmetric pattern filling the field

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a mandala and not Celtic knotwork. No calligraphy bands and no inscriptions; the pattern, the tile and the vault carry the whole image.
