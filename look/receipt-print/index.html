Receipt Print

Also called: thermal receipt print, receipt ticket, till slip

What it is

Images printed on thermal till paper: grainy dithered photos in faded grey-black on bright white slips, item lines, dotted rules, a barcode and a torn serrated edge.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

photo printed on a long thermal receipt: grainy dithered black and white image, faded thermal grey ink on bright white paper, tiny monospace item lines, dotted rules, barcode, torn serrated top and bottom edges

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a cash-register mockup: the picture is the point, and it must look like real thermal print, faded and dithered, not a clean graphic.
