Data Storytelling

Also called: animated chart story, scrollytelling chart, sonified data film

What it is

A chart as the film: real data drawn mark by mark on cream paper by a red-blue pencil, handwritten notes pinned to the numbers, every mark heard as a pitch.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

hand-drawn data chart on warm cream paper, ink dots and lines drawn by a red-blue pencil, handwritten pencil notes pinned to marks, red-to-blue diverging colour ramp, faint dot grid, mono tick labels

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a dashboard, not a keynote with chart slides, never decoration: one idea at a time, and the numbers stay true.
