Clear Water Wallpaper

Also called: turquoise shallows phone background, underwater lock screen, rain-ring water photo

What it is

A tall photographic phone wallpaper of clear turquoise shallows: sunlight caustics, small fish, a mossy branch dipping in, rain rings on the surface, with a calm band at the top for the clock.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

tall 9:19.5 phone wallpaper photograph of clear turquoise shallow water, sunlight caustics, small fish, a mossy branch dipping in from above, rain rings, calm open water at the top, high detail

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a tropical postcard: the water is close and shallow, the palette is one turquoise, and the top third stays quiet.
