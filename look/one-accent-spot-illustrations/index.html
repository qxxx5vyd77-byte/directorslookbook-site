One-Accent Spot Illustrations

Also called: spot illustration, spot illustration set, Japanese cut illustration, line illustration with accent colour, lifestyle spot illustrations, simple line illustration, illustration sheet

What it is

The spot-illustration sheet of Japanese editorial and web design: one character in several small everyday scenes scattered across white, no backgrounds. Thin even black line, flat fills with no shading, one hot pink accent repeated on hair, clothes and props, small touches of pale blue and butter yellow. Tiny comic marks carry the feeling: sweat drops, sparkles, a heart, a puff of motion.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

spot illustration set, one character in several small everyday scenes floating on white with no frames, thin even black line, flat fills, no shading, one hot pink accent on hair and clothes, touches of pale blue and butter yellow, tiny sweat drops and sparkles

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not a comic page and not flat corporate figures: real proportions and expressive faces, a black outline on every shape, no panels and no backgrounds. One accent colour leads; never a full rainbow palette, never gradients.
