Maya Codex Painting

Also called: Maya bark-paper codex, Mesoamerican screenfold painting, Maya manuscript style

What it is

Painting on folded bark paper in the Maya lowlands: figures in strict profile with a fluid black outline, flat fills of red ochre, blue green and black on cream, panels divided by red rule lines, text blocks sitting beside the picture.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

Maya codex painting on bark paper, figures in strict profile with fluid black outline, flat fills of red ochre, blue green and black on a cream ground, panels divided by red rule lines, plain colour panels in the margins, screenfold page

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not Aztec stone relief and not a generic tribal pattern. Never invent glyph text, dates or deities; where glyph blocks would sit, leave plain colour panels. Formal language only.

The details

Era

Type that suits it
Fraunces, Alegreya
Camera native to it
straight top-down on the page, lateral slide as the screenfold opens, macro on a red rule line
