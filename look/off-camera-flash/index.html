Off-Camera Flash

Also called: strobe, speedlight off camera

What it is

One flash off to the side, the ambient underexposed: the subject pops from a darker world with a crisp edge and a deliberate shadow.

What it says: Presence, edge, the editorial frame.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

off-camera flash portrait, single strobe from the side, ambient underexposed one stop, subject lit crisply against a darker background, deliberate hard shadow

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not on-camera flash: the light comes from an angle, not the lens.
