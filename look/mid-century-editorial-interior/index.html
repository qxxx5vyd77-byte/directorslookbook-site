Mid-Century Editorial Interior

Also called: mid-century modern illustration, retro editorial illustration, flat interior scene

What it is

A flat, poster-like interior scene in the mid-century modern manner: elongated poised figures, sunlight drawn as hard-edged flat shafts across walls and floor, pendant lamps and thin-legged furniture, teal, rust orange and cream with no outlines.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

1950s editorial illustration of an interior, flat colour shapes, no outlines, sunlight as hard-edged geometric shafts on the walls, tall poised figure, pendant lamps, thin-legged chairs, teal, rust, mustard and cream

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not Flat Vector's friendly doodles: figures are tall and poised, the light is the main shape, and the palette is muted, never candy.
