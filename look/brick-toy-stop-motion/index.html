Brick Toy Stop-Motion

Also called: brickfilm, toy brick macro, brick stop-motion

What it is

Glossy interlocking plastic bricks with round studs on a real desk, shot with a macro lens and animated a held pose at a time. Toy scale against the real room.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

stop-motion brick film, glossy interlocking plastic toy bricks with round studs on a real wooden desk, macro lens with shallow depth of field, softbox highlights on ABS plastic, red, blue and yellow primaries, contact shadows

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not clay or felt stop-motion (bricks never bend), not a faceted low-poly island, not a spotless product render of a toy.
