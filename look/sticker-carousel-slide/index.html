Sticker Carousel Slide

Also called: instagram carousel, tips post, maximalist sticker layout

What it is

A social carousel slide that looks hand-assembled: chunky black rounded headline with a sticker outline, a list of pastel highlighter pills with arrows, paint-blob shapes, sparkles, asterisks and squiggles, plus a halftone cut-out photo.

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.
