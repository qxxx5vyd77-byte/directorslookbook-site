Chocolate, Violet and Mango

Also called: chocolate ground brights, brown with violet and yellow, talk deck palette, cocoa and candy, brown violet tangerine pink

What it is

A dark chocolate brown as the ground, then four flat brights that sit on it: blue-violet, lemon yellow, tangerine and hot pink. The brown does the work of black but stays warm, so the brights read as candy, not neon.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

dark chocolate brown ground with flat blue-violet, lemon yellow, tangerine and hot pink shapes, brown instead of black for all darks and type, no gradients, no texture

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not black and neon: the ground is brown, not black, and the brights are flat and matte; nothing glows, nothing fades, and white appears only as type.
