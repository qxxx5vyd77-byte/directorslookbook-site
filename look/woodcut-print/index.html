Woodcut Print

Also called: relief print, white-line woodcut, wordless novel plate, woodcut, woodblock

What it is

A relief print that carves itself: a black wood block where light exists only where the knife cut it away. One colour at most, kept for the thing that burns.

The prompt

Paste it into an image or video prompt as is, then add your own subject.

Prompt

woodcut relief print, black ink on warm laid paper, white V-gouge and U-gouge cuts that follow the form, wider cuts in light areas, ragged block edge, one burnt copper-orange colour plate inside the carved whites

Not this

What it gets confused with, or how it fails. Use it as a negative prompt where the tool takes one.

Not this

Not copperplate engraving, not a flat-colour linocut poster, not a photo through a threshold filter: the cuts follow the form.

The details

Era
